About

Omri Ram

Omri Ram is an assistant professor in the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering at the Technion, where he founded the Transient Fluid Mechanics Laboratory in 2021. He trained in shock-wave physics at Ben-Gurion University and, as a Fulbright postdoctoral fellow at Johns Hopkins University, studied how cavitation begins and how oil and water separate after a spill. He is a Zuckerman Faculty Scholar and holds a Career Advancement Chair at the Technion, which has twice recognised his teaching with a commendation for excellence.
