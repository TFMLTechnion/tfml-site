---
title: Dr. Jibu Tom Jose joins our lab
date: 2022-07-31
summary: A postdoctoral fellow arrives from Johns Hopkins.
---
Jibu joins our group as a postdoctoral fellow after graduating from Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore with a Ph.D. and MSE in mechanical engineering. Jibu also holds an M.Tech from the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur and a B.Tech from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham in Coimbatore, India, both in aerospace engineering. His Ph.D. thesis is titled "Experimental investigation of instantaneous velocity and pressure fields over a harmonically pitching airfoil".
