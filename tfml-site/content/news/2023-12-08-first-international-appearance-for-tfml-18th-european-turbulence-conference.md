Dr. Jibu Tom Jose presented our work "On the dynamics of inertial solid particles approaching an area change in a turbulent circular pipe" at the 18th European Turbulence Conference, held in Valencia, Spain, on 4–6 September 2023.

The talk was based on high-speed tomographic PIV and high-resolution stereo PIV data acquired by Dvir Feld, Gal Friedmann and Jibu Jose in the refractive-index-matched water tunnel at TFML.
