02 / A moment, under the microscope

A sphere.
A wake.
A plot twist.

A rising sphere doesn’t just go up. It leaves a wake that pushes back, sending it on a wandering path through the liquid.

These ten experimental snapshots show vortex threads growing, changing shape, and breaking into rings. A whole story in about a fifth of a second.

04 / Meet the experimentalists

Serious science.
Curious people.

We’re a group of engineers and researchers who like our physics hands-on. We design the setup, align the cameras, run the experiment, and ask what we missed.

The lab is led by Omri Ram, an assistant professor of mechanical engineering at the Technion, with a background in shock-wave physics at Ben-Gurion University and experimental fluid mechanics at Johns Hopkins.
